    The unit

    Staff and family use the same rig. They breathe, blink and follow your cursor. Tap one.

    Driven by the simulation

    The rig reads the same numbers the shift engine already tracks. No hand-made animation per event: a falling pressure makes Mrs. Whitfield pale, mottled and hard to rouse on its own.

    Cues you have to notice

    Nothing here is labeled in the room. Tachypnea, diaphoresis, tremor, picking at sheets and tripod posture show up in the drawing, and the student has to connect them.

    Ready for an artist

    Every state here is a named input (pain, SpO₂, alertness). The same list becomes the brief for a Rive artist if the characters move to painted art later.
