The Shift

Privacy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The Shift collects as little as it can to find out what's confusing, what's clinically wrong, and where people stop playing. It doesn't sell data or show ads.

Your game stays on your device

Your shifts, scores, badges and settings are saved in your browser's storage. They aren't uploaded. Clearing your browser data erases them.

What is collected

Usage eventsThings like "started a shift", "finished report", "finished a shift with a score of 74", and the screen you were on if you left mid-shift. Each event carries a random ID made on your device, the app version, a device type (phone, tablet or desktop), and the site or campaign that first brought you here.
ErrorsIf the game crashes, the error message and which screen it happened on, so it can be fixed.
Feedback you sendWhatever you write in a feedback or "Report a problem" form, and the decision it's about. Please don't include patient information or anything identifying about real people.
Your email, if you join the listUsed only to tell you when new shifts ship. It's linked to your random ID so the list can show who actually played.

Not collected: your name, your answers as you type them, your location, or anything from outside this site.

Where it goes

Events and feedback are stored with PostHog, an analytics service, in the United States. Only the person who runs The Shift can see them.

Turning it off

  • In the game: Menu → Settings → Share anonymous usage → Off. Anything not yet sent is deleted.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, usage events are never sent. Feedback and signups you choose to submit still are.
  • To have your email or feedback deleted, or to unsubscribe, email nicholasforell@gmail.com.

Who it's for

The Shift is an educational simulation for adults in nursing education and practice. It isn't a substitute for clinical instruction or your facility's policies, and it isn't meant for children.

Changes

If this changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and the landing page will say so.
