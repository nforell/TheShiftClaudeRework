03 / The difference

The right answer isn't always obvious.

Sometimes the medication is ordered but shouldn't be given. Sometimes the family knows something report missed. Sometimes the patient who seems fine is the one you should worry about.

  • Patients change as time passes and care is delayed.
  • Ask Nova, your charge nurse, for a nudge when you're stuck.
  • Review consequences and clinical reasoning after every shift.

05 / Why this exists

The real test isn't knowing the answer.
It's knowing what to do next.

I'm Nick, a registered nurse with an MSN from Johns Hopkins. I've worked inpatient psychiatry at Johns Hopkins Hospital and in the Bay Area.

The Shift is my attempt to give nursing students and new nurses something a textbook can't: the weight of an assignment, interruptions, changing patients, and the decision that feels small until it isn't.

This is a beta, not a clinically validated training replacement. The patients are fictional. If something is inaccurate or unclear, I want to know — every in-game debrief lets you report a problem.

Nick RN, MSN · CREATOR OF THE SHIFT
Quick answers

Before you clock in.

Do I need an account or to pay?

No. The current beta is free to play in your browser without an account. Your progress saves locally in that browser.

Do I need to be a nurse to play?

No, but the scenarios are written for nursing students and nurses. If you're new, start with the guided orientation and Days on 4 West.

Can I practice NCLEX questions too?

Yes. The game includes unfolding Next Generation NCLEX-style cases, a question bank and rationales for your answers.

Is this suitable as clinical guidance?

No. This is an educational simulation in beta, not clinical advice, a substitute for instruction, or guidance for real patient care. Follow your clinical instructor and facility protocols.
